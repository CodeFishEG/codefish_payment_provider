1. Why this module

InstaPay is how Egyptians pay each other: instantly, from any bank or wallet, with a small fee paid by the sender. But InstaPay has no API for shops. Without this module, customers send screenshots by WhatsApp, someone matches them to orders by hand, and the order waits. This module turns InstaPay into a payment method of your Odoo shop and invoices, with a clear process for your customers and your accountants.

Pay in a few taps

An Open InstaPay button on phones, your QR code on computers, and Copy buttons for the amount, the reference, the numbers and the IBAN. No typing mistakes.

Receipts in the right place

Customers upload the screenshot on their own payment page, with its reference. It lands in a review list with the order, the amount and a deadline, not in a chat.

A promise you keep

"We confirm within 48 working hours": the customer sees the exact date, counted on your working schedule, Fridays, Saturdays and public holidays skipped.

One click to confirm

Confirm, and Odoo confirms the order, posts the payment in the right bank journal with the InstaPay reference, and reconciles the invoice. Or reject with a reason, and the customer is asked again.

Fake receipts caught

The same screenshot or reference on two orders, an amount that differs, a transfer dated before the order: each is flagged, and a reference is accepted only once.

No order left waiting

A reminder after a day, then the payment is cancelled after the delay you choose, with a link to pay again. A late transfer can still be confirmed.

2. How it works

#WhoWhat happens
1CustomerChooses InstaPay at checkout, on an invoice or a payment link. The order waits for the transfer.
2OdooShows the amount, the order reference and your receiving accounts on the confirmation page, in the portal and by email.
3CustomerSends the money from the InstaPay app (or their bank or wallet app), then uploads a screenshot of the receipt with its reference.
4OdooTells the customer the date of the confirmation, and gives your reviewer an activity due on that date.
5AccountantFinds the transfer in the statement of the receiving account, then confirms the payment, or rejects the receipt with a reason.
6OdooConfirms the order, posts the payment, reconciles the invoice and emails the customer. Unpaid orders get a reminder, then are cancelled.

3. What your customers see

  • At checkout, InstaPay with its logo and a short note: your company confirms the payment within the review time.
  • The payment details: the exact amount and the reference with Copy buttons, then one card per receiving account. InstaPay accounts show an Open InstaPay button on phones and your QR code on computers; wallets and banks show their numbers and IBAN with Copy.
  • The payment page, opened from Upload your receipt (or by scanning its QR code to continue on the phone): the screenshot, the reference, the amount and the date, already filled in.
  • After the upload: "Your company will confirm your payment by Monday 16 November", and the same date by email.
  • Pay another way, until a receipt is sent: the InstaPay payment is cancelled and the customer can pay with another method.

Every page and email speaks the customer's language, Arabic included, and names your company.

4. Reviewing receipts

Each receipt gives your reviewer an activity, due on the date the customer was promised. The Invoicing › Customers › InstaPay Receipts menu lists the receipts to review, most urgent first, overdue ones in red, with filters for possible duplicates, amount differences and receipts added by your team. The kanban view shows the screenshots.

The receipt shows the screenshot next to the declared reference, amount, date and account, the amount to pay and the order date, and a reminder: never confirm from a screenshot or an SMS alone, find the transfer in the statement of the receiving account.

  • Confirm Payment: choose the account where you found the money and the amount received, and tick that you found it in the statement. The amount must cover what is due.
  • Reject Receipt: choose a reason; the message to the customer is filled in, in their language. The customer gets an email and can send a new receipt, unless you also cancel the payment.
  • Add Receipt on a payment, when a customer sends the proof by WhatsApp or email: it is reviewed like the others and marked as added by your team.

5. Working days and holidays

The review time (2 working days by default) is counted in working hours on your working schedule, and public holidays are skipped. A receipt sent on Thursday at 15:00, on a Sunday-to-Thursday 9:00–17:00 schedule, is due on Monday at 15:00. With our free Egyptian localisation (l10n_eg_cf_hr_holidays), the official holidays, as moved by the Prime Minister's decisions, are skipped too.

The provider warns you when the schedule works on Fridays or the company has no time zone, and when nobody would receive the review activities.

6. Set-up guide for administrators

  1. Install InstaPay Payments. It creates the InstaPay provider, its payment method, an InstaPay bank journal in EGP and an InstaPay Reviewers role with your account managers.
  2. Open Invoicing › Configuration › Payment Providers › InstaPay.
  3. Add your receiving accounts in the Receiving Accounts tab:
    • InstaPay: in the InstaPay app, open your profile, share your payment link (https://ipn.eg/S/…) and export your QR code; paste the link, upload the QR code, add your mobile number;
    • Mobile Wallet: Vodafone Cash, e& cash, Orange Cash, WE Pay or a bank wallet, and its number;
    • Bank Account: the bank, the account number and/or the IBAN (checked);
    • for each one, the beneficiary name customers see in their app, and the Odoo journal of that account.
  4. Set the review time, the working schedule and the reviewers in the InstaPay group of the Configuration tab.
  5. Choose when to remind customers and when to cancel unpaid payments.
  6. Publish the provider.

8. Accounting

  • The payment is posted in the journal of the account where the money arrived (your bank or wallet journal), so it matches its statement; without one, in the InstaPay journal created at installation.
  • Its memo is the InstaPay reference, its date the date received, and the screenshot is attached to it.
  • Odoo reconciles the invoice, as for any online payment.
  • You receive the full amount: InstaPay's fee is paid by the sender.
  • InstaPay transfers can't be reversed: refunds are a credit note and a transfer from your app.

10. Security and fraud checks

  • Statement check: confirming requires ticking "I found this transfer in the statement of the receiving account".
  • Warnings for a screenshot or reference already sent for another payment, an amount that differs, a transfer dated before the order or in the future, a receipt added by staff.
  • One reference, one payment: an InstaPay reference can be accepted only once per company.
  • Evidence kept: screenshots and declared details can't be changed; accepted receipts can't be deleted.
  • Safe uploads: PNG or JPEG only, at most 10 MB; every image is decoded and saved again, without its metadata (location, phone) or anything hidden in it. SVG, PDF and HTML files are refused.
  • Private payment pages: each has a secret link tied to the payment and the customer; never cached or indexed; at most 10 receipts per payment, one every 30 seconds.
  • Checked accounts: only real InstaPay links (https://ipn.eg/S/…), Egyptian mobile numbers and valid Egyptian IBANs are accepted, so a typo never sends money elsewhere.
  • Access: Billing and Accountant users review; read-only accountants see; multi-company rules apply.

11. Questions and troubleshooting

Does InstaPay notify Odoo of the transfer?No: InstaPay has no API for shops. Your team confirms each payment from the statement of the receiving account; the module organises everything around that.
Can the link or QR code carry the amount?No: they are fixed for your account. The customer types the amount shown with a Copy button; the reference on the receipt links the transfer to the order.
Customers can't type our InstaPay addressRecent versions of the app don't allow it. Add your payment link and QR code: customers open or scan them.
Orders above 70,000 EGP?InstaPay's limit per transfer. Raise the provider's Maximum Amount if you accept several transfers: the page tells customers to send them and upload each receipt.
InstaPay doesn't show at checkoutCheck it is published, has an active receiving account, and the order is in EGP and below the Maximum Amount.
A confirmed receipt doesn't confirm the orderOdoo's scheduled actions must run: the decision is applied by the payment processing cron within seconds.
Can we use our bank and wallet journals?Yes: set the journal on each receiving account (EGP). Payments are posted where the money arrived.
Will customers see "Odoo"?No: payment pages and emails name your company.
