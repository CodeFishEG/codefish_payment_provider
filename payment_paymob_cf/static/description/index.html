1. Why this module

Paymob takes cards, mobile wallets, cash at kiosks and buy-now-pay-later in five countries. This module connects your Odoo shop, your invoices and your payment links to Paymob, keeps every payment in sync with Paymob, and lets your team refund, capture and follow payments from Odoo.

Every Paymob method

Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Meeza, mada, OmanNet, Jaywan), wallets, kiosk, bank installments, valU and 8 more installment brands, Tabby, Tamara, STC Pay, JazzCash, Easypaisa, Apple Pay and Google Pay, each with its logo.

Five countries

Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman and Pakistan, each on its own Paymob platform and in its own currency. Pick the country of your Paymob account; the rest follows.

Pay without leaving your site

Paymob Checkout opens in a pop-up, a side panel or a full page. With the embedded integration, the card form opens right on your payment page.

Saved cards, capture, refunds

Customers save their cards and pay again in a few clicks. Your team authorizes and captures card payments, voids them and refunds them, fully or partially, from Odoo.

Always up to date

Paymob's signed notifications, the customer's return, a live waiting page and a check every 15 minutes confirm each order as soon as it is paid, even when a notification is missed.

Every code explained

Declines and errors are explained: your team sees what the code means and what to check, your customers see what to do, and never the details of your account.

Payment split

Share each payment with the merchants connected to your Paymob account, for all items or only some products, for example a marketplace's sellers.

Accounting ready

A "Paymob" bank journal in your account's currency is created when you install the module. Paid orders are posted in it, apart from your other banks.

Arabic, right to left

Every text is translated into Arabic, the layout follows right-to-left websites and your theme's colours, and Paymob's card form follows the customer's language.

And Odoo's own Paymob connector? Odoo 20 includes a basic Paymob connector that sends the customer to Paymob's checkout. This module is independent of it: install it with or without Odoo's connector. It adds:

  • Pakistan, and every method of the five countries;
  • the embedded card form, pop-up and side-panel displays;
  • saved cards, merchant-initiated charges (MOTO), authorize and capture, void, full and partial refunds;
  • refund, capture and void notifications, kiosk references, status checks, payment split, response codes explained;
  • its own bank journal, test data for administrators, and Arabic.

It never renames anything in your Paymob account.

3. Two integrations

Choose on the provider how your customers pay. You can switch at any time; payments already started keep working.

Paymob Checkout

The customer picks a method in Odoo, then pays on Paymob's secure checkout, which opens directly on that method: card with 3-D Secure, wallet, kiosk reference or installments.

Best for: the quickest start, with every method of your account.

Embedded checkout

Paymob's secure card form opens on your payment page. Cards can be saved, captured manually and charged again with Paymob's server APIs. Wallets, kiosk and installments use Paymob Checkout.

Best for: saved cards, manual capture and a payment that stays on your website.

4. What your customers see

The Paymob methods appear at the payment step of your shop, on invoices in the customer portal and on the payment links you send.

Paying by card

  1. The customer chooses Card and clicks Pay now.
  2. Paymob's secure card form opens on the page (embedded checkout), or Paymob Checkout opens (pop-up, side panel or full page). The card details are typed there, never on your website.
  3. The customer confirms the payment with their bank (3-D Secure).
  4. Back on your website, the order is confirmed: "Your payment has been processed."

Paying in cash at a kiosk

  1. The customer chooses Kiosk; Paymob Checkout gives a reference number.
  2. The confirmation page and the order in the customer's portal show the reference number with a Copy button, the amount and the steps to pay.
  3. The customer pays the reference in cash at a payment kiosk; the page updates by itself and the order is confirmed.

Wallets and installments

With a mobile wallet, the customer enters their wallet number on Paymob Checkout and approves the payment on their phone. With valU, Souhoola, Tabby, Tamara or bank installments, the customer chooses the plan on Paymob Checkout; the order is paid in Odoo as usual.

5. Saved cards

With the embedded checkout, signed-in customers can save their cards and pay the next orders in a few clicks. Card numbers are typed in Paymob's form only: Odoo keeps Paymob's card token, the brand, the last four digits, the expiry date and the name on the card.

  • Save a card while paying: the customer ticks Save my payment details; Paymob saves the card with the payment and sends it to Odoo.
  • Save a card without paying: from My Account › Payment Methods, when your Paymob account has a Verification integration.
  • Pay with a saved card: the customer picks the card at checkout; Paymob Checkout opens with that card, and the customer confirms it with its security code and the bank.
  • Charges while the customer is away (for example subscriptions): Odoo charges the saved card through your MOTO integration.
  • Remove a card: the customer clicks the bin in My Payment Methods. Paymob offers no API to delete saved cards: the card is removed from Odoo and can't be used any more. Expired cards are removed by themselves.

To turn it on: choose Embedded checkout on the provider and tick Allow Saving Payment Methods. Paymob Checkout can't save cards for Odoo.

6. Authorize, capture and void

Reserve the amount on the customer's card when the order is placed, and take it when you ship. Ask Paymob for an Auth card integration, enter its ID on the provider and tick Capture Amount Manually.

  • Card payments wait as Authorized in Odoo.
  • Capture the whole amount or a part of it from the order, the invoice or the transaction.
  • Void an authorization you won't capture; Paymob releases the amount on the card.
  • Paymob voids authorizations that aren't captured within 14 days.

Odoo only captures manually when every active payment method of the provider supports it: turn off wallets, kiosk and installments on this provider, or use a second Paymob provider for them.

7. Payment split

Share each payment with merchants connected to your Paymob account, for example the sellers of a marketplace or a delivery partner. Paymob pays each merchant its share; the rest is yours. Nothing changes for the customer.

  • Share (%) of each item: the merchant receives this part of the price of every item it applies to.
  • Applies to all items, or only to selected products.
  • Odoo sends the shares with every payment and refuses settings that would share out more than 100% of an item.

To turn it on: ask Paymob to connect the merchants and enable split amounts, tick Payment Split on the provider and add each merchant, with the merchant ID Paymob gave you, in the Payment Split tab.

8. Set-up guide for administrators

Plan about 15 minutes. You need the Administration / Settings access right.

Before you start

  • A Paymob account. Its dashboard has a test mode and a live mode, each with its own keys and integration IDs.
  • Your Odoo website reachable from the internet over HTTPS, so that Paymob can bring customers back and send its notifications.
  • Prices in the currency of your Paymob country on your website pricelist.
  • The Invoicing app (installed with eCommerce) with a chart of accounts, for the Paymob journal.

1Install the module

In Apps, search for Paymob Payments and click Activate. The installation:

  • adds the Paymob provider, in test mode and unpublished, set to your company's country when Paymob serves it;
  • adds the payment methods and turns on Card, Mobile Wallet and Kiosk;
  • creates the Paymob bank journal (in Arabic "مدفوعات باي موب") in each company, in the currency of its Paymob country;
  • adds the scheduled action Paymob: check pending payments, which runs every 15 minutes.

2Enter your keys

  1. Open Invoicing › Configuration › Payment Providers (or Website › Configuration › Payment Providers) and click Paymob.
  2. In the Configuration tab, choose the Paymob Country of your account.
  3. In Paymob's dashboard, test mode, open Settings › Developers › API Keys, and copy the Secret Key, the Public Key, the API Key and the HMAC Secret into Odoo. Leave Live unticked.
  4. Click Test Connection: Odoo checks the API key and counts the integrations of your account.

The Configuration tab with the embedded checkout.

3Choose the integration and the display

  • Paymob Integration: Paymob Checkout or Embedded checkout (see Two integrations).
  • Checkout Display: Full page, Pop-up window or Side panel, for Paymob Checkout.
  • Payment Expiry (Hours): how long a payment, such as a kiosk reference, can be completed. 48 hours by default.
  • Allow Saving Payment Methods (embedded only), and the MOTO and Verification integration IDs if Paymob created them for you.

4Link your Paymob integrations

Paymob gives each payment method of your account an integration ID (dashboard: Settings › Payment Integrations). Click Sync Integrations: Odoo reads your integrations and fills the ID of each payment method, and the Auth, MOTO and Verification IDs it recognizes. Nothing is changed in your Paymob account. You can also type an ID on a payment method.

5Notifications

Odoo sends its Notification URL (ending with /payment/paymob_cf/webhook) with every payment, so Paymob notifies Odoo when a payment changes. You can also set it as the Transaction processed callback of each integration in Paymob's dashboard. Odoo checks every notification with your HMAC secret.

6Publish and test

While the provider is unpublished, its methods show on your website only to your own team: place a few test orders. The Test Data tab of the provider lists Paymob's published test cards and test wallet, with links to Paymob's webhook tester and HMAC checker.

The Test Data tab, shown in test mode.

7Go live

  1. Tick Live (this also publishes the provider) and replace the four keys with the live ones of Paymob's dashboard, then save.
  2. Click Sync Integrations again: live integrations have other IDs than test ones.
  3. Click Test Connection.
  4. Place one small real order and refund it from Odoo.

10. Daily use for your team

Your sales and accounting team works with Paymob payments like with any other Odoo payment. Here are the Paymob-specific tasks.

Find a payment

From the sales order (Payments smart button), the invoice, the provider (Transactions) or the Paymob journal. The transaction's Paymob tab shows Paymob's order ID and reference, the integrations used, the card or wallet paid with, the kiosk reference, the installment plan and the last status check. When Paymob refused the payment, it also shows the code, what it means and what to check. Paymob's transaction ID is the transaction's Provider Reference.

A declined payment: the code, what it means, and Paymob's message.

Check a payment at Paymob

A customer says they paid, but the order still waits? Open the transaction and click Check Paymob Status. You rarely need it: Odoo already checks pending payments every 15 minutes.

Refund a payment

  1. Open the payment (Invoicing › Customers › Payments) and click Refund.
  2. Keep the full amount or type a smaller one, then click Refund.
  3. Odoo sends the refund to Paymob and records it. If Paymob refuses it, Odoo shows why and records nothing.

You can refund several times, up to the amount paid. Kiosk payments can't be refunded through Paymob; Tamara and Premium Card only in full.

Capture or void

With manual capture, authorized payments show Capture Transaction and Void Transaction buttons on the order, the invoice and the transaction.

Send a payment link

Use Odoo's Generate a Payment Link (action menu of orders and invoices). The customer opens it and pays with any Paymob method.

Saved cards

The Tokens smart button of the provider lists the saved cards (last four digits only), with their expiry date and the name on the card. Archiving a card in Odoo removes it for the customer.

TypeBank

CurrencyThe currency of the provider's Paymob country; it follows the country while the journal has no entries
Incoming payment methodPaymob, linked to the Paymob provider
  • When Paymob confirms a payment, Odoo posts a customer payment in the Paymob journal and reconciles it with the invoice.
  • The balance of the journal is the money Paymob collected for you and hasn't transferred yet. When Paymob settles it, record an internal transfer to your bank, and Paymob's fees as a bank fee.
  • A journal that already uses the code PAYMB is reused; reinstalling never creates a second one. Uninstalling keeps the journal and its entries.

  • the customer's return to your website, after which Odoo asks Paymob for the status;

14. Security

  • No card numbers in Odoo. Cards are typed in Paymob's checkout or Paymob's card form only. Odoo keeps Paymob's card token, the brand, the last four digits and the expiry date.
  • Signed notifications. Every notification and return from Paymob is checked against its HMAC-SHA512 signature, and matched with its payment by the order Paymob signs. Odoo asks Paymob for the status of a payment before trusting a return.
  • 3-D Secure on card payments made by the customer, saved cards included.
  • Protected pages. Payment pages and status checks are tied to the payment by a secret token; one customer cannot see another's payment.
  • Restricted keys. Only administrators see the secret key, the API key and the HMAC secret. Keys and card tokens never appear in the logs.
  • Safe copies. When a database is duplicated for testing (neutralized), the Paymob keys are removed, so a copy can never take real payments.

15. Questions and troubleshooting

The Paymob methods don't show at checkout.

  • Is the provider Published, and are the methods turned on in its Payment Methods tab?
  • Is the website pricelist in the currency of your Paymob country?
  • Wallets, kiosk and installments are offered to customers of the countries they serve.
  • Do the provider's Restrictions (amount, countries, websites) allow the order?

Payments fail with "error integration" or "error missing_integration".

The payment method has no Paymob integration ID, or one of the other mode (test or live). Click Sync Integrations, or type the ID shown in Paymob's dashboard (Settings › Payment Integrations) on the payment method.

Test Connection says Paymob rejected the connection.

Check the four keys, without spaces, and that they belong to the provider's country and mode: test keys work only with Live unticked.

The customer paid, but the order is still waiting.

Click Check Paymob Status on the transaction. If it happens often, check that your website is reachable over HTTPS, that the HMAC secret is right, and that the scheduled action Paymob: check pending payments is active.

Customers can't save their cards.

Saving cards needs the Embedded checkout and Allow Saving Payment Methods, and the customer must be signed in. Saving a card without paying also needs a Verification integration.

I can't tick Capture Amount Manually.

Enter the Auth Integration ID first, and turn off the methods that can't be captured manually (wallets, kiosk, installments) on this provider.

Can saved cards be charged automatically, for subscriptions?

Yes, with the embedded checkout and a MOTO integration, which Paymob creates on request.

How do I test kiosk payments and installments?

Paymob has no simulator for them, and bank installments, Apple Pay and Google Pay only work with live integrations: test them with small live payments, then refund them where possible.

Can I keep Odoo's own Paymob connector?

Yes. The two providers are independent; publish the one you want your customers to use.

What happens if I uninstall the module?

The Paymob provider is unpublished and its keys removed. The Paymob journal, its payments and the transactions stay.
